I. CORE ARCHITECTURAL AXIOMS & DISCOVERIES


1. THE TELECOMMUNICATIONS EQUIVALENCE OF MIXTURE-OF-EXPERTS (MoE):
   Prior art treats transformer Mixture-of-Experts architectures (specifically 
   Grok-1: 314B total parameters, 64 cascade layers, 8-way FFN experts per stage, 
   Top-2 gating) as high-dimensional, polysemantic mathematical black boxes. 
   This invention establishes that an MoE transformer operates identically to 
   a switched RF distribution plant:
   - Input context tokens act as incoming high-frequency carrier signals.
   - Attention heads, RMSNorm, and gating routers act as active line-conditioning 
     gear (equalizers, automatic gain controllers, and directional couplers).
   - The 64 transformer layers represent an optical trunk cascade.
   - The 8 Feed-Forward Network (FFN) experts per layer represent switched 
     directional tap drops terminating in demodulating end-points (modems).
   - Top-2 gating constitutes an autonomous time-division crossbar switch 
     where only 25% of the drop lines carry active RF power per cycle, while 
     75% remain dark copper.

2. HIGH-IMPEDANCE DIRECTIONAL PROBE TAP (ZERO-IO IPC):
   Traditional model telemetry imposes synchronous evaluation or heavy disk I/O, 
   inducing latency jitter and causing page-cache thrashing. 
   The deployed probe introduces a high-impedance directional test-point 
   borrowed from RF amplifier test ports (-20 dB directional couplers).
   By isolating the Top-2 gating router output into an atomic, 24-byte binary 
   record within POSIX shared memory (/dev/shm), the probe extracts routing 
   decisions in <1 microsecond without line-loading the inference execution pipeline.

3. PHYSICAL PLANT "TOMBSTONE" AUDITING & CIRCUIT SLICING:
   Routing telemetry is drained asynchronously via an out-of-band archivist 
   into a structured SQLite engine (catalog.db under WAL mode) and compiled 
   into an Obsidian physical plant switchboard (577 discrete nodes: 1 Headend, 
   64 Trunks, 512 Tap Endpoints).
   Every expert endpoint note serves as an electronic equipment tombstone 
   recording:
   - Line condition (dark_copper vs. active_carrier vs. saturated_trunk)
   - Real-world duty cycle percentage
   - Top-2 Co-activation bridge links (bonded RF trunks)
   - Domain-specific semantic tags
   This provides deterministic mechanistic interpretability (a verifiable 
   physical route slip per token) and proves an empirical pruning pathway: 
   dark copper experts firing below critical thresholds are cleanly severed, 
   compressing 314B frontier weights into specialized sub-networks capable 
   of resident execution on edge unified-memory hardware (Jetson AGX Orin 64GB).

4. EMPIRICAL BARE-METAL TRANSPORT VERIFICATION:
   Validated on the sovereign node "logos" via unbuffered Direct I/O (O_DIRECT) 
   and page-aligned double-buffered memory mappings across PCIe Gen4 NVMe:
   - Line Throughput: 2,711.23 MB/s (3,072.00 MB in 1.13 seconds).
   - Page Cache Footprint Delta: +0.20 MB total across multi-gigabyte ingestion.
   - Proves zero page-cache gorging and complete immunity against Tegra 
     BCCPLEXWDT hardware watchdog panics caused by synchronous kswapd direct reclaim.
